Regressions

Two scans of one chatbot, test case by test case: what got better, what broke.

What is a regression, and why look case by case?

A regression is something that used to work and broke after a change. This page catches those by comparing two scans of the same chatbot question by question, not just by overall score.

Why overall scores aren't enough. Say Scan 1 and Scan 2 both average 0.90. That looks like nothing changed, but underneath 3 questions the bot used to answer correctly may now fail, while 3 others that used to fail now pass. The average hides both; this page shows exactly which questions went which way.
